Merged Datasets: Bird Radar + Open-Meteo Weather
==================================================

Files:
  train_with_openmeteo.csv  –  train.csv enriched with hourly weather
  test_with_openmeteo.csv   –  test.csv  enriched with hourly weather

Generated by: join_openmeteo_weather.py

Source weather data: Open-Meteo historical archive API (ERA5 reanalysis).
Fixed query coordinate: 53.44°N, 6.83°E (Windpark Eemshaven, Groningen).
All tracks are within ~2 km of this point, well inside one grid cell.

Join method: Each radar track's temporal midpoint is matched to the most
recent Open-Meteo hour using a backward asof join (tolerance: 1 hour).
Open-Meteo timestamps are hour-start (09:00 covers the 09:00–10:00 interval),
so midpoint 09:40 maps to the 09:00 row.

Added columns:
  openmeteo_matched_timestamp_utc            – matched Open-Meteo hour (UTC)
  openmeteo_air_temperature_2m_c             – air temperature at 2 m (°C)
  openmeteo_relative_humidity_2m_percent     – relative humidity at 2 m (%)
  openmeteo_dew_point_2m_c                   – dew point temperature at 2 m (°C)
  openmeteo_precipitation_mm                 – total precipitation in the hour (mm)
  openmeteo_cloud_cover_percent              – total cloud cover (%)
  openmeteo_cloud_cover_low_percent          – low-level cloud cover, below ~2 km (%)
  openmeteo_cloud_cover_mid_percent          – mid-level cloud cover, 2–6 km (%)
  openmeteo_cloud_cover_high_percent         – high-level cloud cover, above ~6 km (%)
  openmeteo_pressure_msl_hpa                 – mean sea level pressure (hPa)
  openmeteo_surface_pressure_hpa             – actual surface pressure (hPa)
  openmeteo_weather_code                     – WMO weather code (0=clear, 1–3=cloud, 51+=drizzle/rain)
  openmeteo_wind_speed_10m_kmh              – wind speed at 10 m (km/h)
  openmeteo_wind_direction_10m_degrees       – wind direction at 10 m (degrees, 0=N, 90=E)
  openmeteo_wind_gusts_10m_kmh              – maximum wind gusts at 10 m (km/h)
  openmeteo_shortwave_radiation_w_m2         – total shortwave radiation at surface (W/m²)
  openmeteo_direct_radiation_w_m2            – direct (beam) solar radiation (W/m²)
  openmeteo_diffuse_radiation_w_m2           – diffuse (scattered) solar radiation (W/m²)
  openmeteo_sunshine_duration_s              – seconds of sunshine in the hour (0–3600)
  openmeteo_vapour_pressure_deficit_kpa      – vapour pressure deficit (kPa); higher = drier air
  openmeteo_is_day                           – 1 if daytime, 0 if nighttime
  openmeteo_match_time_difference_minutes    – minutes between track midpoint and matched hour

Notes:
  - Visibility was requested but returned 100% null; it is not included.
  - soil_temperature_0cm and CAPE were also unavailable for this grid cell.
  - All remaining columns have 0% null across both train and test.
